# Operating System

- `ONE FACT = ONE GROUND TRUTH`。
- `UPSTREAM DECIDES → DOWNSTREAM CONSUMES → DO NOT RE-JUDGE`。
- AI 负责 Semantic Judgment、Business Judgment、Reasoning、Explanation；程序负责 Math、Join、Dedup、Filter、Aggregation、Coverage、Schema、Identity、Timestamp、Latest、Read-back。
- `Keyword Market Fact != Benchmark Observation`；同一 Keyword 的市场容量只计算一次。
- `Relevance != Precision`；`Precision != Market Size`；`Benchmark Organic Rank != Precision`。
- `Intent != Campaign`；`Shared = Validation`；`Independent = Control`。
- `Daily Analysis != Daily Modification`；`NO DECISION WITHOUT REASON`；`NO ACTION` 也必须是有原因的正式决策。
- `Organic Search Occupancy != Sales Market Share`。

不得把单指标、字符串相似、排名数量、搜索量、机会比或运行天数直接替代经营判断。
