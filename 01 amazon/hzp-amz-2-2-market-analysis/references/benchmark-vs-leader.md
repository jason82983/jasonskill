# Benchmark vs Leader

## Three-level model

| Level | Role | Main question |
|---|---|---|
| Benchmark | 当前对标产品或对标新品 | 为什么它值得作为开发起点？ |
| Leader | 当前 Niche 榜1或成熟头部代表 | 市场已经验证了什么成功方案？ |
| Market | 整体 Niche | 消费者真正购买什么，竞争和机会如何？ |

Use one ASIN data package per ASIN even when an ASIN carries multiple roles. Do not duplicate a complete 2-1 analysis for the leader; reuse a dated 2-1 report when it exists and focus 2-2 on market entry implications.

## Triangle validation

Answer four questions:

1. **Market** — what demand and search intent are actually purchased?
2. **Leader** — what mature solution wins, at what price and evidence level?
3. **Benchmark** — what did it copy, change, or solve, and is its growth reproducible?
4. **Our Opportunity** — what should HZP keep, improve, avoid copying, and validate?

The decision chain is:

Market Need → Leader Solution → Benchmark Innovation / Weakness → Unmet Need → HZP Development Opportunity

Compare product form, core use, price, review growth, keywords, promotions/ads, recurring pains, return risk, and operational constraints. A benchmark weakness alone does not prove a market opportunity.

## New or fast-growing benchmark

Check sustained history, natural keyword coverage, review growth against the sales signal, rating deterioration, price/promotion dependence, and emerging consumer problems. If the history is short, mark the conclusion [持续性待验证].

## Benchmark equals leader

If the benchmark is the current leader, mark Benchmark = Leader and do not duplicate the analysis. Add a second mature representative only when a specific comparison question requires it.
