# Evidence Priority

## Priority order

1. Amazon market behavior and current Niche evidence.
2. Precisely matched third-party data with matching marketplace, date, ASIN/Niche, and metric definition.
3. User-selected market labels or manual notes.
4. AI interpretation.

A lower-priority source cannot override a higher-priority contradictory source without showing the conflict and reason.

## Evidence labels

- [数据支持] — directly supported by a source file, page snapshot, or explicit user confirmation.
- [分析推断] — interpretation derived from one or more supported facts.
- [待验证] — requires a current export, prototype, supplier confirmation, or user decision.
- [证据不足] — the data exists but cannot be matched or is insufficient for the claim.
- [样本有限] — review or feedback sample is too small to represent the whole market.

Do not upgrade [分析推断] or [待验证] to fact without new evidence.
