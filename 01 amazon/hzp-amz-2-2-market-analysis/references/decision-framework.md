# Decision Framework

## Gates

1. Market scope is matched and not based on a name alone.
2. Demand is real enough for the intended entry market.
3. Competition leaves an identifiable entry path.
4. Consumer gap can be translated into a product requirement.
5. Price position is worth further development.
6. Risks and validation tasks are explicit.
7. In Benchmark-Driven Mode, the benchmark's key strength is worth keeping and each core improvement is market-validated.

## Verdicts

- GO: market quality, demand, entry conditions, and a supported benchmark-based improvement opportunity are sufficiently supported.
- CONDITIONAL GO: the market is valuable, but named development, cost, price, validation, or differentiation conditions must be met before development or investment.
- NO-GO: current evidence shows poor demand, blocked competition, no credible market-wide gap, non-reproducible benchmark success, or unattractive risk/return.
- HOLD / STOP: P0 evidence is missing or mismatched; do not force a final verdict.

A conditional verdict must list measurable conditions, owner or evidence source, and validation method. Do not use “可以考虑” as a final decision.
