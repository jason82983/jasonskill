# 阶段 5｜消费者未满足需求分析（消费者证据辅助规则）

本文件提供消费者反馈、退货与需求地图的证据整理规则；阶段 5 的完整执行顺序、六项分析、结论标签、优先级和商业信号边界见 [stage-5-unmet-consumer-needs.md](stage-5-unmet-consumer-needs.md)。阶段 6 负责将已确认的 P0/P1 需求验证为产品切入机会，本文件不直接设计产品方案；阶段 7 负责最终市场进入决策。

## 证据来源

Combine Niche positive reviews, negative reviews, return insights, benchmark Reviews, leader Reviews, listing claims, and search-term intent. Keep each observation tied to its source and ASIN/Niche/date where available.

## 编码地图

- 购买动机
- 满意点
- 反复出现的痛点
- 退货原因
- 权宜做法或未满足需求
- 上下文：使用场景、人群、尺寸、材料、安装或耐久性

Separate frequency from severity. A vivid individual comment is not a market consensus. Mark small or biased samples [样本有限].

## 机会链

For each candidate gap, write:

证据 → 消费者问题 → 市场缺口 → 机会 → 验证

Validation may require an updated Niche export, review sample, prototype test, packaging drop test, supplier confirmation, or launch experiment.

## 对标产品改良检查

For each benchmark pain, record whether it is repeated in Niche feedback, whether the leader solves it, and whether it can change purchase, rating, return, use-scene, audience, or price behavior. A pain observed only in the benchmark should remain benchmark-specific [待验证] until market evidence confirms it.
