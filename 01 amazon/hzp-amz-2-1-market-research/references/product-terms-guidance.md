# Product terminology guidance

Use this reference for all Amazon product categories.

## Rule
For important English category, material, function, construction, compatibility, use-case, or consumer-problem terms that matter to the decision, show:
- English term
- IPA pronunciation
- concise Chinese meaning

Do not create a fixed vocabulary list for every category. Terms should come from the product title, Cerebro keywords, reviews, Keepa context, supplier context, or other provided evidence.

## Selection priority
Prefer terms that help QMT understand:
1. the category and product form;
2. the core consumer demand;
3. the functional promise;
4. material/construction differences;
5. compatibility, size/capacity, or use scenario;
6. important Amazon search-language distinctions.

## Accuracy
- Do not invent technical definitions.
- If pronunciation is uncertain, use a reliable standard IPA form or omit IPA rather than guessing.
- Preserve category-specific terminology. Do not translate two materially different English terms into the same Chinese phrase without explaining the difference.
- Claims such as waterproof, food-safe, medical, child-safe, load-bearing, fire-resistant, non-slip, or certified must not be treated as verified performance unless evidence supports them.
